A console-based digital pet system with:
State variables:
- hunger
- energy
- mood

User actions:
- feed
- play
- rest
- check status
- exit

Rules:
- hunger increases over time
- playing reduces energy
- low energy forces rest
- high hunger decreases mood
- values must stay within limits

safe boundaries:
- hunger: 0 to 10
- energy: 0 to 10
- mood: 0 to 10

values must never:
- go negative
- exceed max
- become inconsistent

Questions:
- what happens if hunger reaches 10?
- what happens if energy reaches 10?
- can mood become negative?
- what happens if user keeps playing?
- when does simulation end?
